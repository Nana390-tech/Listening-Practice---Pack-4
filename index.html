Before you start — what the test makes you spell

In a listening test the marker does not care whether you understood. They care whether the word on the page is spelled correctly. One missing letter and the mark is gone. The same small family of words comes up again and again:

days & monthssurnames spelled outprices & numbers reference codesplaces on a campustravel & booking words

Names and codes are different: nobody expects you to know them, so the speaker spells them out. Your only job is to catch the letters in order. Everything else comes down to six traps:

Double lettersacomodationaccommodation
Silent lettersreciptreceipt
Syllables you never hearlabratorylaboratory
Endings that sound the sameinsurenceinsurance
Vowels in the wrong orderTeusdayTuesday
French endingsbrocherebrochure
